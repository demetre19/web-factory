Childcare Cleaning Experts

We understand that children touch everything, and with dirty hands, which is why our childcare cleaning services in Melbourne, we prioritize frequently touched areas to prevent cross-contamination and maintain a good visual presentation.

Floors are especially challenging to keep clean as children often spill food, paint, or track in sand from the sandpit. Given the floor is the largest surface, keeping it clean is a significant task.

Our childcare cleaning approach is collaborative. We work alongside your team, complementing the ongoing tidying and cleaning by your educators throughout the day.

Our Childcare cleaning service covers children’s rooms, staff rooms, reception area, and, of course, the most essential toilets – for both children and adults.

We intentionally focus on thorough vacuuming and mopping to remove paint, food residue, and sand.

What kind of cleaning products do you use?

90% of our cleaning products are eucalyptus oil-based, while the remaining 10% consist of ethanol for glass cleaning and enzymes for toilet bowls.

Are your cleaners police checked?

Yes, all team members are police-checked. All our childcare cleaners hold current Working with Children Checks, National Police Clearances, and complete our specialized childcare cleaning certification. We regularly update their training in infection control.

Are your cleaners fully insured?

Yes, all team members are fully insured.
