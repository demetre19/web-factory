Our Founding Story

A story that explains why we operate this way and a journey that uncover the values we stand for.

  • 2014

    Begin

    I was ashamed I am a cleaner when I first started this business. Not because Im a cleaner, but because my parents work so hard their whole life so that I can come to Australia for my further studies. Despite life given me the best circumstances yet thats the best I can do.

    After dedicating myself to turning around a struggling company, I was made redundant just one week before becoming full-time. I felt betrayed, unjust, and frustrated. The corporate environment I’d worked in was filled with mismanagement and politics. Work is supposed to be empowering.

  • 2018

    Discover

    Fast forward a decade later, fate had granted me a platform to create a profound impact in the lives of cleaners through Whistle Clean. 

    In the process of founding Whistle Clean, I juggled four to five jobs simultaneously. I sold cameras by day, worked in a pizza restaurant at night, and cleaned offices after midnight. Despite the long working hours, I felt a sense of happiness and freedom because I was doing what I loved.

  • 2020

    Values

    This business was built by door-knocking around South Melbourne, Richmond, Collingwood, and Port Melbourne. Throughout this journey, I drew inspiration from fellow cleaners. Often, when I asked, “Why do you work 16-18 hours a day?”. Their answers were often heartfelt:

    “I want to buy a walking stick for my elderly mum with my first pay.”
    “I want my kids to have a better education.”
    “I want to send money back home so my parents can have a better quality of life.”

  • Today

    Growth

    Whistle Clean’s purpose then became to create an uplifting environment for cleaners, empowering them to lead better lives. Through this journey, I learned that people harbour untapped potential, and the human spirit is limitless. My passion for personal development became the driving force behind Whistle Clean, serving as a channel for empowering individuals. This has been an uplifting and inspiring journey, which I am deeply grateful for.

Whistle Clean Australia is a Melbourne-based commercial cleaning company delivering office, medical clinic, childcare, end-of-lease, carpet steam, and window cleaning services. Founded in 2014, the company focuses on reliability, proactive communication, and consistent quality, assigning dedicated cleaners and conducting regular 90-day check-ins.

Whistle Clean uses 90% eucalyptus oil-based, biodegradable cleaning products and plants one tree for every cleaning visit through its partnership with Tree Nation, reflecting a strong commitment to sustainability.

The company operates with no lock-in contracts and emphasizes values of love, trust, growth, integrity, and contribution, aiming to empower cleaners and foster respectful work environments. Clients range from corporate offices to healthcare and childcare centers, with testimonials highlighting high standards, responsiveness, and ethical practices
