    Whistle Clean Australia is a Melbourne-based commercial cleaning company delivering office, medical clinic, childcare, end-of-lease, carpet steam, and window cleaning services. Founded in 2014, the company focuses on reliability, proactive communication, and consistent quality, assigning dedicated cleaners and conducting regular 90-day check-ins.

    Whistle Clean uses 90% eucalyptus oil-based, biodegradable cleaning products and plants one tree for every cleaning visit through its partnership with Tree Nation, reflecting a strong commitment to sustainability.

    The company operates with no lock-in contracts and emphasizes values of love, trust, growth, integrity, and contribution, aiming to empower cleaners and foster respectful work environments. Clients range from corporate offices to healthcare and childcare centers, with testimonials highlighting high standards, responsiveness, and ethical practices
